Iron Commissar

Privacy policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Iron Commissar ("the App") is a kettlebell training tracker developed by dddruwu. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and email address for authentication purposes only.
  • Workout data: Training sessions, routines, exercises, and personal records you create within the App.
  • Device information: Basic device information collected by Google AdMob for ad serving.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and sync your workout data across devices
  • To display personalised advertisements via Google AdMob
  • We never sell your personal data to third parties

4. Data Storage

Your workout data is stored locally on your device and synced to secure cloud servers associated with your account. Data is protected by row-level security policies. All data is encrypted in transit.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: authentication
  • Google AdMob: advertising
  • RevenueCat: purchase management
  • Supabase: cloud data storage

6. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings screen within the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The App is intended for users aged 18 and above.

8. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, open the Iron Commissar app, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data from our servers. Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing dddruwu@gmail.com. Requests will be processed within 30 days.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: dddruwu@gmail.com
